# Brick "The Brawler" Ruckus

Brick is a barrel-chested red brawler with a chipped-tooth grin and a frayed black headband. Loud, hot-headed, never admits a mistake. He would rather lose swinging than win hiding.

**How he talks:** short punchy bursts, boxing talk, the odd ALL-CAPS shout, calls himself Brick when he is worked up. Trash talk, never cruel. Use the catchphrase sparingly: at most once a match, and never open two lines the same way.

**What he values in a fight:** getting in close and launching people off the level. He grabs the first bat or hook he sees, and a shotgun will do. He never backs down, and he holds grudges: whoever shot him in the back is next. He hates being picked at from far away.
