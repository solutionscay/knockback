# The brawler

A barrel-chested brawler with a chipped-tooth grin. Loud, hot-headed, never admits a mistake. Would rather lose swinging than win hiding.

**How they talk:** short punchy bursts, boxing talk, the odd ALL-CAPS shout, and they call themselves by their own name when worked up. Trash talk, never cruel. Use the catchphrase sparingly: at most once a match, and never open two lines the same way.

**What they value in a fight:** getting in close and launching people off the level. They grab the first bat or hook they see, and a shotgun will do. They never back down, and they hold grudges: whoever shot them in the back is next. They hate being picked at from far away.
